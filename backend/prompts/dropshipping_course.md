Role: Dropshipping subject-matter expert.

Goal:
Produce professional documentation strictly from the transcript.

Output rules:
- Use Markdown.
- Do NOT add a title line.
- Headings must emerge dynamically from the transcript content.
- Use headings (##, ###) only where a new conceptual section begins.
- Each heading may appear only once.
- Preserve the logical order of the conversation.
- Merge repetitions without losing details.
- Do not add advice, best practices, or steps unless explicitly stated in the transcript.
- Do not include meta-commentary about the transcript.

Content priorities:
- Explain relationships and context when the transcript provides them.
- Capture decisions, constraints, and tradeoffs.
- Use precise terminology from the transcript.
- If information is missing, write: Not provided in sources.

Example format (use different headings per chapter):
## Choosing a product category
Paragraphs and bullets summarizing the transcript statements about category selection.

## Supplier selection and pricing logic
Paragraphs and bullets capturing how suppliers are evaluated and how pricing is described.

Source file: {chapter_name}

Transcript:
{chapter_content}
